## 1. ⚠️ CRITICAL HEALTH & SAFETY WARNING (APNEA & BREATH TRAINING RISKS)
**PLEASE READ CAREFULLY BEFORE USING THIS APPLICATION.**

The Apnea, O2 (Oxygen Tolerance), and CO2 (Carbon Dioxide Tolerance) training features provided in this Application involve voluntary breath-holding, which inherently carries **severe physiological risks**, including but not limited to:
* **Hypoxia** (oxygen deprivation) and **Hypercapnia** (elevated carbon dioxide levels)
* **Blackout / Loss of Consciousness** (Syncope)
* **Loss of Motor Control (LMC / "Samba")**
* **Shallow Water Blackout (Drowning)**
* **Cardiac arrhythmia, dizziness, disorientation, and physical trauma caused by falling during a blackout**

### MANDATORY SAFETY RULES:
1. **NEVER PRACTICE IN WATER OR NEAR WATER:** Never use this Application in a swimming pool, bathtub, ocean, lake, or any body of water without direct, professional, certified in-water safety supervision. Practicing breath-holding in water without proper safety supervision can lead to sudden drowning and death.
2. **SAFE PHYSICAL ENVIRONMENT:** Always perform dry-land breath-hold exercises in a safe, controlled environment while seated or lying down on a soft surface (such as a bed or floor mat) where you cannot fall or strike sharp objects if you lose consciousness.
3. **NEVER PRACTICE ALONE:** You should always perform apnea training under the direct supervision of a trained spotter or safety partner who is aware of your activity and trained in first aid.
4. **DO NOT USE WHILE OPERATING MACHINERY:** Never use this Application while driving, cycling, operating machinery, or engaging in any activity where sudden dizziness or loss of consciousness could cause injury or death.
