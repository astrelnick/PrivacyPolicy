# Privacy Policy for Guitar Tutor
**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

Thank you for choosing **Guitar Tutor** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (*Guitar Tutor*) handles information when you use our services.

## 1. Overview and Core Principle
**Guitar Tutor is designed with privacy-first principles.** The app is an educational music practice companion that provides interactive tablature lessons, pitch detection feedback, a chromatic guitar tuner, a precision metronome, and daily practice habit tracking. 

We do **not** collect, sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information.

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Personal Information
We **do not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, financial information, or government identification. You can use all features of the application without registering an account.

### B. Practice Progress and Local Data
Your practice statistics, daily practice goals, minutes practiced today, completed lesson list, and user preferences (such as interface language: English, Ukrainian, German, or Russian) are stored **locally on your device** (using device local storage / `localStorage`). This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to any external server.

### C. Microphone and Audio Processing (`RECORD_AUDIO`)
Guitar Tutor requests access to your device's microphone solely for:
- **Interactive Practice Feedback:** Analyzing guitar notes and chords played in real-time to provide immediate timing and accuracy feedback during interactive lessons.
- **Chromatic Guitar Tuner:** Measuring audio frequencies to display tuning accuracy and pitch cents gauge for Standard Tuning (E-A-D-G-B-E).

> **Important:** Audio data is processed **ephemerally in real-time in device memory (RAM)**. The app **never** records, saves, stores, or transmits your audio or voice to any external server, cloud storage, or third party.

## 3. Device Permissions
The app may request the following permissions on your Android device:

- **`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`**: Used strictly for on-device real-time pitch detection and tuner operation as described above. Audio is never saved or transmitted.
- **`android.permission.INTERNET`**: Used to load fonts, interactive tab libraries (AlphaTab), and web assets.
- **`android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS`**: Used for WebAudio playback features such as metronome clicks and synthesized lesson audio tracks.

The app does **not** access your location, camera, contacts, photo library, call logs, or device storage files outside the app's sandboxed environment.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** Guitar Tutor does not display third-party advertisements and does not integrate ad network tracking SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network).
- **No Behavioral Tracking:** We do not track your activity across third-party applications or websites.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)
Our application is intended for a general audience of music learners, students, and guitar enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app is safe for users of all ages.

## 6. Data Security and Retention
Because your practice progress and settings are stored locally on your device, you retain complete control over your data:
- You can reset or delete all stored data at any time by clearing the application's cache/storage in your device's Android settings (`Settings > Apps > Guitar Tutor > Storage > Clear Data`).
- Uninstalling the application will automatically remove all locally stored preferences and progress data.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)
Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR or California under CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal data:
- Because Guitar Tutor does not collect, process, or store personal data on remote servers, there is no personal data stored by us to access, rectify, port, or erase.
- You maintain complete ownership and control over your locally stored data on your device.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or application features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
