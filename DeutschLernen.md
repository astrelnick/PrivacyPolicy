# Privacy Policy for DeutschLernen
**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

Thank you for choosing **DeutschLernen** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (*DeutschLernen*) handles information when you use our services.

## 1. Overview and Core Principle
**DeutschLernen is designed with privacy-first principles.** The app is an educational language learning platform designed to help users learn German across CEFR levels A2 through C2. We do **not** collect, sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
We **do not** collect any personal data such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, financial information, or government identification. You can use all core features of the application without registering an account.

### B. Learning Progress and Local Data
Your lesson progress, quiz scores, and settings (such as chosen interface language: English, German, Ukrainian, or Russian) are stored **locally on your device** (using device local storage). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly connect to a self-hosted or dedicated backend server.

### C. Audio and Text-to-Speech (TTS)
DeutschLernen utilizes on-device / operating system Text-to-Speech (TTS) engines to play pronunciation audio for vocabulary, stories, and dialogues. The app **does not record**, process, or transmit your voice or microphone input.

## 3. Device Permissions
The app may request the following standard permissions:
- **`INTERNET`**: Used strictly to load dynamic educational assets, lesson materials, or communicate with the application server if hosted online.
The app does **not** access your location, camera, contacts, photo library, microphone, or device storage files outside the app's sandboxed environment.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** DeutschLernen does not display third-party advertisements and does not integrate ad network tracking SDKs (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network).
- **No Behavioral Tracking:** We do not track your activity across third-party apps or websites.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR)
Our application is intended for a general audience (students, professionals, and language enthusiasts). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate measures.

## 6. Data Security and Retention
Because your learning progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, you retain full control over your data. You can completely erase all stored learning data at any time by clearing the application's storage/cache in your device's Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)
Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR or California under CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal data. Because DeutschLernen does not collect, process, or transmit personal data to remote servers:
- You have automatic total control over your local data.
- There is no personal data to access, rectify, port, or erase on our servers.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or application features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
